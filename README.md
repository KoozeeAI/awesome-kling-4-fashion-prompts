## 🧠 What Is Kling 4.0?

Kling 4.0 is Kuaishou's video generation model, officially launched on October 15, 2026. Kuaishou describes stronger continuity for high-speed action and complex camera movement, native single-pass video generation of up to 30 seconds, Omni Reference with up to 15 reference assets, and control with up to 10 keyframe inputs.

### Common Fashion Use Cases

- Runway films and model movement
- Fashion lookbook video and outfit transitions

- E-commerce and social media marketing videos
