# Olive Velvet Handbag Studio Commercial

A curated AI fashion prompt for Handbag · Fashion Accessory Commercial · Video.

## Prompt

Create a new video where, in a sunlit white studio, a woman in an Etro paisley suit takes an olive-green velvet handbag from the mouth of a full-size jet-black winged horse — a Pegasus whose huge black feathered wings stay clearly visible on its back the entire time — then she and the Pegasus turn and walk off together to the right side of the frame, side by side, leaving the room empty; only then does the Etro logo, a black winged-horse emblem above the word ETRO, snap onto the white wall. One continuous, locked-off wide shot; regal, surreal, high-end fashion-campaign finish, soft window daylight from the left. ONE single woman and ONE single horse, no clones. Her face, hair, build and outfit stay exactly as in the starting frame. The horse stays the SAME full adult size, the same build and the same huge wings in every single frame — it never shrinks, never becomes a foal or pony, and its wings never disappear. [SCENE 1] THE STARTING FRAME: the woman stands in the left third in the window's light, turned toward the black Pegasus, her right hand reaching for the olive velvet bag that hangs by its handle from the horse's mouth; the full-size horse stands just to her right, neck arched, head lowered, its huge black feathered wings raised high. THE SETTING (UNCHANGED FROM FRAME): the white cyclorama wall, seamless white floor, the tall window on the LEFT and its diagonal daylight shapes remain static, and the back wall stays completely blank and white. The frame is locked; no camera movement. Her fingers close around the bag's rounded velvet handle, the horse opens its mouth and releases it, and she draws the bag in and hooks it over her forearm, smiling. The wings lower to a half-raised position, swept back above the horse's body, every long black feather still clearly visible. [SCENE 2] Both of them turn to face RIGHT — the woman turns her body to the right, and the Pegasus turns its body around to face right — THEN they walk forward together toward the right edge of the frame, side by side, same direction, the horse in a calm, proud four-beat walk with its head nodding, its big half-raised black wings riding high on its back and lifting gently with each stride, clearly visible against the white wall the whole way; the woman strides beside it with the bag on her arm and her flared paisley trousers swinging. Both walk forward naturally, facing the way they travel, NOT backward, not sliding, not drifting. The horse and its wings stay full-size as they walk out past the right edge. Throughout this walk the back wall stays completely blank — no logo, no mark, no shadow of one. [SCENE 3] Only once both of them are fully out of frame and the white studio is completely empty, the logo appears on the centre of the back wall all at once, instantly, no fade: a solid black leaping winged-horse emblem centred above the word "ETRO" in wide, letter-spaced black capitals. A still, held hero end frame, the window daylight still falling across the white wall and floor.
