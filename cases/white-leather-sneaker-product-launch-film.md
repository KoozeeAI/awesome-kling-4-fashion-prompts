# White Leather Sneaker Product Launch Film

A curated AI fashion prompt for Shoes · Sneaker product launch commercial · Video.

## Prompt

16:9, 10 seconds, sound on. One continuous FPV shot, no cuts. Video 1 is the camera reference: copy its camera path, speed ramps and timing, but none of its content. Image 1 is the product: a white leather sneaker with a gum sole. Look: photorealistic product launch at IMAX scale. A 14mm FPV camera, bright volumetric light, a seamless white studio floor. 0-2s: The camera races low across the white floor as scraps of white leather, laces and gum-rubber pellets lift off and float upward. 2-4s: The camera rockets up through spinning leather panels and a rising sole, near-misses on both sides. 4-6s: It climbs beside the shoe as the sole locks in and the upper wraps around it. 6-8s: A fast orbit as stitching runs along the seams in a wave and the laces thread themselves. 8-10s: The last eyelet clicks in, and the camera pulls back to reveal the finished sneaker standing alone on the white floor, exactly like Image 1. Sound: deep bass, leather snapping taut, a sharp click on every lock, wind rushing past the lens. Negative prompt: buildings, a construction site, logos, text, people, a different shoe.
