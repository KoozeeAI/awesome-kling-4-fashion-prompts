# Paisley Jacquard Suit Campaign Walk

A curated AI fashion prompt for Full Outfit · Fashion Campaign · Video.

## Prompt

Create a new video where a confident woman in an Etro paisley suit walks into an empty, sunlit white studio from the right side of the frame, crosses the floor toward the tall window on the left, and settles into a poised standing pose in the window's pool of daylight, gazing back toward the open right side of the room as if expecting someone. One continuous, locked-off wide shot; bright, airy, premium fashion-campaign look, soft window daylight from the left. ONE single woman, no clones. The woman: an Italian woman in her early thirties, slim-athletic build, warm olive skin with an olive undertone, dark brown hair in a low loose chignon with a few strands framing her face, natural real skin, minimal makeup; she wears a single-breasted jacquard blazer with a teal, emerald and oxblood paisley motif on a charcoal ground with a black velvet collar, worn buttoned, flared high-waisted black chenille-jacquard trousers with a teal, turquoise and oxblood paisley motif, and black pointed-toe ankle boots. The outfit stays exactly the same in every frame. [SCENE 1] THE STARTING FRAME: the empty white studio — white cyclorama wall and seamless white floor, the tall window on the LEFT edge throwing diagonal daylight shapes across the left wall and the floor. No one in the room. THE SETTING (UNCHANGED FROM FRAME): the wall, floor, window and light patches remain static; only the woman moves. The frame is locked; no camera movement. The woman steps in from just beyond the RIGHT edge of the frame, already facing left, walking forward toward the window side with long, confident, unhurried strides, the flared trousers swinging with each step, heels landing naturally, her arms swinging lightly. She walks forward naturally, facing the way she travels, NOT backward, not sliding, not drifting. [SCENE 2] She arrives at her spot about one-third in from the left edge, standing in the window's light patch on the floor, small and full-length in the wide frame. She slows, plants her weight on her left leg, turns her body slightly back toward the open right side of the room, slips her right hand into the trouser pocket, lifts her chin and looks off toward the right, calm and poised — a held, settled final pose with gentle breathing and a faint shift of weight. The soft window daylight from the left lights her face and the paisley jacquard, her shadow falling to the right across the white floor; the background stays bright near-white.
